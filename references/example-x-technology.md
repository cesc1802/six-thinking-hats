# Worked example — "X Technology" (workbook pp. 16–18)

Use this to calibrate depth and to show the user what a completed session looks like.

## Context
A smartphone maker, "X Technology", has low revenue. It wants a high-quality new model that is innovative, matches user expectations, stays cost-effective, and breaks through in the market.

## Problem statement
While remaining cost-effective, the company must develop a new phone with innovative features that meets consumer needs and can stand out in the market.

## Hats as applied in the workbook (order: White → Black → Yellow → Red → Blue → Green)

**White — data**
- Collected: market trends, current pricing, competitor product specs, consumer behaviour.
- Finding from validated data: users value camera, battery life, and convenience most.

**Black — risk**
- Potential technical barriers; capital pressure from R&D; latent market risk.
- Finance warns: if the sales forecast is wrong, the company faces cost overruns and cash-flow problems.

**Yellow — opportunity**
- Pioneering features can attract tech enthusiasts and the premium segment.
- Marketing: with accurate positioning and promotion, the product could become the new industry benchmark.

**Red — feeling**
- Designers are excited about the innovative features; engineers are worried about implementation difficulty.
- Market staff's intuition: a new product will trigger social-media discussion that drives sales.

**Blue — control**
- The project lead sequences the discussions so they stay orderly and productive.
- The Blue group consolidates conclusions, plans milestones, and defines execution steps.

**Green — ideas**
- Modular design, eco-friendly materials, fast charging.
- Integrate health-management features; use AI to improve user experience.

## Resulting direction (Blue close)
1. Superior camera system and ultra-fast charging as the main differentiators.
2. Recyclable materials to strengthen the eco attribute.
3. Health tracking and smart assistance built in.
4. Modular design so users can customise features to personal need.
5. Personalised marketing focused on the unique selling points and innovative features.

## What to notice when calibrating
- The Blue close turns every hat's output into a line item: White's "users value camera/battery" → direction 1; Green's ideas → directions 2–4; Yellow's marketing thesis → direction 5. Black's cash-flow warning is *not* visible in the close — in a real session that is a gap; the action plan should carry a financial gate (e.g. forecast validation before tooling spend). Point this out when using the example as a template.
